Workflow & Accountability

How CivicTrack Resolves Issues

A clear, SLA-driven transparent workflow from citizen submission to verified on-ground completion.

1
1. Citizen Reports

Submit issue details with automatic GPS location and upload photo evidence from your phone or PC.

2
2. Auto-Routing & SLA

System assigns the grievance to the respective ward officer and generates a guaranteed SLA resolution countdown.

3
3. Field Crew Action

Municipal field inspectors and repair crews are deployed on-site to inspect, execute, and verify repairs.

4
4. Verified Resolution

Officers upload proof of work. Citizen receives instant SMS/Email notifications to confirm satisfactory closure.

About The Initiative

Re-engineering Municipal Grievance Redressal

CivicTrack is an official open-governance initiative engineered to modernize public grievance management. By eliminating bureaucratic delays, manual paperwork, and lack of transparency, CivicTrack ensures that every civic concern voiced by a resident is tracked, acted upon, and resolved within guaranteed timeframes.

Guaranteed Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

Strict resolution windows ranging from 48 hours for hazardous potholes to 7 days for street repairs.

Photographic Proof of Work

Municipal officers must upload geo-tagged resolution photos before a complaint can be formally closed.

Executive Oversight & Accountability

City administrators monitor overdue tickets and departmental efficiency in real-time dashboards.

Citizen Charter Commitment
Standardized Public Service Delivery
  • No Registration Required to Track: Anyone with a tracking code can inspect public status.
  • Automated Escalation: Unattended complaints are auto-escalated to senior department directors.
  • Direct Citizen Feedback: Rate municipal services upon grievance resolution.
