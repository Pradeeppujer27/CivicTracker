Karnataka Emergency Helplines

CivicTrack handles non-emergency municipal civic issues. For live wire hazards, medical distress, or disaster emergencies, contact official Karnataka 24x7 helplines directly:

  • Unified Emergency: 112
  • BESCOM / Power Control: 1912
  • KSDMA Flood & Disaster: 1077
  • Janaspandana Helpline: 1902
